# Campfire Tales

A small roleplay addon for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. Rest at a campfire and gain its buff, and a popup offers a short "Did you know..." lore line. Click **Say** or **Yell** to tell it to everyone nearby.

*Every fire has a story.*

## Features
- Popup appears automatically when you gain a campfire buff (at most once every 5 minutes)
- One-click **Say**, **Yell** or **Another** (new line)
- 46 built-in lore lines
- Add your own lines with `/campfact add <text>`
- Free and open source (MIT)

## Commands
- `/campfact` shows a random line
- `/campfact say` or `/campfact yell` speaks a line directly
- `/campfact auto on|off` turns the automatic popup on or off
- `/campfact add <text>` adds your own line (max 255 characters)
- `/campfact learn` shows the buff names you gain, and `/campfact watch <id or name part>` adds one to the trigger list

## Why a click?
Forever requires a hardware event (a click or typed command) to send /say and /yell, so the addon offers a one-click popup.

## Install
Download from [Wago Addons](https://addons.wago.io), or copy the `CampfireTales` folder into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns` and `/reload`.

Compatibility: WoW: Forever, interface 16001.
